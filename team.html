The Team

Students run this team. Mentors keep it standing.

Every decision that reaches the robot is made by a student — the drivetrain architecture, the autonomous strategy, the budget that pays for it. Mentors teach, review, and hold the safety line. They do not build the robot.

We compete under gracious professionalism: we lend parts to the team we are about to play, and we mean it.

Five subteams, one machine

Students specialize the way an engineering firm does. Every subteam is led by a student and backed by mentors from industry.

Design

Turns a rulebook into a plan: prototypes, full-robot CAD, and the drawings the build runs from.

CAD · PROTOTYPING · DRAWINGS

Mechanical

Builds what Design draws — drivetrain, manipulators, and structure, on mills, lathes, and a lot of iteration.

MACHINING · FABRICATION · ASSEMBLY

Electrical

Power distribution, motor controllers, sensors, and the pneumatics that have to survive a match.

WIRING · SENSORS · PNEUMATICS

Programming

Autonomous routines, driver controls, and vision. Java on the roboRIO, version-controlled like real software.

JAVA · CONTROLS · VISION

Non-Tech (Business)

The budget, sponsor relationships, media, and the community programs that carry the team's name.

FINANCE · MEDIA · OUTREACH
