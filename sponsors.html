Sponsorship

Put your name on a machine fifty students build.

Team 41 runs on sponsorship. Registration, raw stock, the control system, tooling, and travel for a full roster come to five figures a season, and nearly all of it comes from companies and individuals who decide this is worth funding.

In-kind support

Machine time, raw stock, electronics, software licenses, or printing. If you make it or stock it, we can probably use it.

Mentorship

An engineer for one evening a week during build season is worth as much to us as a check, and often more.

Matching gifts

Many employers match educational donations. Ask us and we will supply whatever paperwork your program needs.
