WHEAT Foundation

The nonprofit behind the team.

Team 41 competes under the Watchung Hills Education and Technology (WHEAT) Foundation — the 501(c)(3) that makes the season financially and organizationally possible.

Who WHEAT is

WHEAT is an all-volunteer organization made up primarily of parents of Team 41 students. It's a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit that raises funds and provides the organizational and logistical support the team needs to design, build, and compete each season.

Working alongside the school's faculty advisors and team mentors, WHEAT handles the parts of running a robotics program that fall outside the shop — fundraising, sponsor relationships, travel logistics, and the finances that keep fifty students building every winter.

501(c)(3) nonprofit

Donations to WHEAT on behalf of Team 41 are tax-deductible.

All-volunteer, parent-run

Run by the families of Team 41 students, alongside faculty advisors and mentors.

Funds the season

Fundraising and sponsorship dollars raised through WHEAT fund the design, build, and operation of the robot every year.
