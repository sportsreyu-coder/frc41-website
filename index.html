Who We Are

Team 41 is a student-driven robotics team dedicated to inspiring young people to become science and technology leaders. Every build season, we design, fabricate, and program a robot to compete in the FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC), guided by the values of gracious professionalism and coopertition.

Beyond competition, we're committed to STEM outreach in our community — mentoring younger teams, hosting workshops, and sharing our passion for engineering with the next generation.

Mission

To inspire students in science, technology, engineering, and math through hands-on robotics.

Values

Gracious professionalism, teamwork, curiosity, and giving back to our community.

Our Team

We're made up of student engineers, programmers, strategists, and outreach leaders, supported by dedicated mentors and coaches.

Mechanical

Designs and builds the physical robot, from drivetrain to game-piece mechanisms.

Programming

Writes the code that brings the robot to life — autonomous routines and driver controls.

Electrical

Wires and maintains the robot's electrical systems, sensors, and controls.

Outreach & Business

Manages sponsorships, community events, and tells the story of our team.
