FIRST Robotics Competition · Founded 1997

A student engineering program that ships a robot every spring.

Every January a new game is announced. Six weeks later fifty students deliver a 120-pound machine they designed, machined, wired, and programmed themselves, then compete with it through April.

Sponsorship pays for the shop, the materials, and the travel. In return, our sponsors put their name on a machine seen by thousands of families, schools, and companies each season.

I · The program

Students make the engineering decisions. Mentors teach and review.

Every decision that reaches the robot belongs to a student: the drivetrain architecture, the autonomous strategy, the budget that pays for it. Mentors from industry teach the tools, review the designs, and hold the safety line. They do not build the robot.

We compete under gracious professionalism, the FIRST principle that you lend a part to the team you are about to play. Off the field, students mentor younger teams and run workshops across our community.

Five subteams, one machine.

IV · The season

One game, six weeks, one robot.

FIRST Robotics Competition runs on a fixed calendar. For anyone who has not seen a season up close, this is the shape of what sponsorship pays for.

JANUARY

Kickoff

FIRST releases a new game to every team on the same morning. No one sees it in advance.

SIX WEEKS

Design and build

Students prototype, CAD, machine, wire, and program a 120-pound robot from raw stock and a control system.

MARCH — APRIL

Competition

Regional events pair three-team alliances against each other. Strong seasons advance to the World Championship.

YEAR ROUND

Training and outreach

Training new members, mentoring younger teams, and running workshops across the community.
